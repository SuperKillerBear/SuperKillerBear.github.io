Privacy policy

Redline · last updated 20 October 2026

Redline is a personal app used only by its owner. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

With permission, Redline reads and writes the owner's Google Calendar events, so they can be shown, created, moved and deleted from within Redline.

Where data goes

Calendar data is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Redline's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections, or with Disconnect in Redline's settings, which also deletes the stored sign-in token.
